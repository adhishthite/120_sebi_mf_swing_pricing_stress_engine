# Frontend Design Specification: SEBI Mutual Fund Swing Pricing Stress Engine

This document details the frontend visual identity, design token parameters, and structural layout for the SEBI Mutual Fund Swing Pricing & Outflow Stress Test Cockpit (Project ID `120`).

---

## 1. Design Read & Vibe Language

State the One-Line Design Read:
> **Reading this as: Risk Management & Stress Testing Cockpit for AMC Fund Managers and Compliance Officers, with a high-density, trust-first visual language, leaning toward Cloud Design System (Carbon/Pantheon).**

### Design System: Cloud Design System (CDS / Carbon / Pantheon)
To replicate a professional cloud console / trading dashboard:
- **Spacing:** 4px atomic spacing (`gap-1` is 4px, `gap-2` is 8px, `gap-4` is 16px).
- **Border Radius:** Minimal corners (`rounded-sm` = 2px, `rounded-md` = 4px). Avoid overly rounded pills for dashboard panels.
- **Grids & Dividers:** Clean thin borders (`border-zinc-200` in light, `border-zinc-800` in dark). Monospace typography for numbers.
- **Colors (OKLCH):** High-contrast status badges:
  - `CLEARED` (Compliant): Muted Green
  - `BLOCKED` (Statutory Block): Sharp Orange/Red
  - `HELD` (Needs Human Review): Gold/Yellow
  - `SWUNG` (Swing Pricing Active): Deep Purple/Indigo (Signaling policy action)

---

## 2. The Three Dials

* **`DESIGN_VARIANCE`: 4** (Symmetric, aligned grid cards, predictable, trust-first, zero slop)
* **`MOTION_INTENSITY`: 3** (Restrained transitions, instant data updates, micro-feedback on buttons, zero cinematic animations)
* **`VISUAL_DENSITY`: 7** (Cockpit feel. Dense tables, compact metric lists, monospace font for ledgers and calculations)

---

## 3. Signature Element: Interactive Liquidation Waterfall Canvas

Every demo requires a memorable interactive feature:
* **Interactive Liquidation Waterfall & Stress Canvas:**
  - A dashboard section containing a slider representing simulated "Total Outflow (Redemption) Request".
  - As the user drags the slider (e.g., from ₹0 to ₹500 Crores against a ₹1,000 Crore AUM fund), the UI renders a real-time horizontal bar-chart representing the asset classes.
  - The bar chart animate-reduces sequentially (Cash $\rightarrow$ G-Sec $\rightarrow$ Corp AAA $\rightarrow$ AA $\rightarrow$ High Yield) to show *which* assets are sold first.
  - Below the bar chart, a real-time line chart/gauge displays the accumulated **Liquidation Cost** (in INR) and the resulting **Swing Factor** (in %).
  - A horizontal dashed line marks the SEBI swing pricing trigger threshold (e.g., 5% outflow for discretionary or market dislocation status for mandatory). Once crossed, the gauge turns purple, indicating the NAV has swung.

## 4. 2-Step Workspace Layout

### Step 1: Onboarding & Configuration
- A panel to select the **Scheme PRC (Potential Risk Class) Category** (e.g. A-III High Risk, B-II Medium Risk, etc.) and configure the fund's **AUM (Asset Under Management)**.
- A toggle for **Market Dislocation** (declared by SEBI) and **Discretionary Swing Threshold** configuration.
- A button to proceed to the simulation cockpit.

### Step 2: Split-Screen Executive Workspace
- **Left Panel (Presets & Scenario Trigger):**
  - High-clarity preset buttons representing different stress scenarios:
    - `SCEN-A` (Golden Path): Single small compliant redemption.
    - `SCEN-B` (Retail Exemption): Redemption under ₹2 Lakh (remains unswung even during stress).
    - `SCEN-C` (Discretionary Breach): Large redemption in normal times, triggering discretionary swing.
    - `SCEN-D` (Market Dislocation Mandatory): Market dislocation active + High Risk PRC category, triggering mandatory full swing pricing.
    - `SCEN-E` (DPDP Aadhaar/PAN Masking): Input containing raw Aadhaar and PAN strings, demonstrating gateway redaction.
    - `SCEN-F` (Non-Exempt Scheme): Sovereign/Gilt scheme category check (exempt from swing pricing).
  - An interactive form to manually construct a transaction (amount, PAN, Aadhaar).
- **Right Panel (Walkthrough & Storyboard):**
  - **Storyboard Walkthrough:** A 3-step visual sequence of the current run:
    1. *Gateway Compliance:* Displays inputs and statutory CEL checks (Exemption status, DPDP masking).
    2. *Liquidation Simulation:* Shows the interactive waterfall chart, computed liquidation cost, and the PRC matrix lookup.
    3. *Audit & Explanation:* Renders the unswung vs. adjusted NAV metrics, transaction status badges, and the Gemini 3.5 Flash Liquidation Explainer text card.
  - **Real-Time Audit Ledger:** Renders the raw JSONL audit trace entries generated by the backend server via Server-Sent Events (SSE).
